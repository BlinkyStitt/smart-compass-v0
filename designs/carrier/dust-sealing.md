# USB-C pigtail and dust protection

Run a short captive lead down from the rear cover. The external head is the
USB-C charging inlet. It contains the input controller and current gate.
Only a switched 5 V pair passes through the case. The usual wall cable
plugs into the head. Fit its tethered cap when the cable is unplugged.

## Cable exit

The Rev A rear cover fit model fills the four old 6 mm vents and adds a
12 mm opening at X 75 mm, Y −44 mm. The opening has a 2.4 mm thick flat
bearing land. Fit a **Heyco M4448** M12 right-angle cordgrip with its integral
panel seal and supplied locknut. Point the elbow down from the lower-right rear of the case. The lead hangs
below the bottom edge. Use a round,
continuous cable jacket within the gland's 1.7–5.3 mm range. Use the maker's
instructions for assembly and check the supplied parts against the cover.
The manufacturer rates the cordgrip IP68 when installed as specified.
[Heyco cordgrip data](https://www.heyco.com/products/liquid-tight-cordgrips/nylon-cordgrips/heyco-tite-liquid-tight-cordgrips-right-angle-metric-hubs/), [locknut dimensions](https://www.heyco.com/products/liquid-tight-cordgrips/nylon-cordgrips/heyco-nylon-locknuts-metric-thread-2/)

Aim for 100 mm of lead below the elbow. Use 22 AWG power and return
conductors with a round jacket. Feed the precrimped ends through the gland
and fit the keyed housing on the inside. Leave 25 mm of internal handling
slack to unplug the charger with the rear cover supported. Clamp the intact
jacket. Keep the stripped conductors and connections inside. Seal the head
housing around its own cable exit and retain its receptacle mechanically.
A cable clip below the pendant can support the wall lead while it is plugged
in.

The CAD checks the cover, cordgrip and charger envelopes. Check the exact
gland, nut, wire bend, plug, cap, and hand access on the first assembly. The
STEP output is a fit part.

## Close the other dust paths

| Path | Seal for the prototype |
| --- | --- |
| Rear cover seam | Use a one-piece silicone gasket on a continuous flat land. Give the fasteners even compression with hard stops. Use sealed washers on any through fastener that crosses the gasket boundary. |
| Battery door | Use a replaceable perimeter gasket and even latch compression. Keep the pull ribbon entirely inside the seal. Check contact around both hinge corners and at the latch with the keeper and door fully seated. |
| Front lens and glow ring | Give each exposed seam a continuous silicone seal or a continuous bonded joint. Keep the gasket outside the visible light path. |
| Buttons and disconnect | Use sealed panel controls with their mounting seals. Fit an approved sealed boot and panel gasket if retaining the toggle. Check the underside and fastener paths. |
| Cable passages, strap mounts and fastener pockets | Inspect each opening for a path into the cavity. Close unused cable passages and keep strap hardware in blind features or outside the seal. |
| Printed surfaces | Inspect for cracks, pinholes and layer gaps. Check that the gasket lands are flat and continuous. Verify the finished print as part of the assembly. |

## Verify the assembled case

Target **IP6X** for the complete assembled case under IEC 60529. Test the
actual print, seals, fasteners, controls, cell door and gland. Test with
the wall cable installed and with the external USB-C head capped. Cycle
the door, controls and cable before repeating the dust check. Open the
case in a clean area and inspect inside the electronics and optical cavity
for dust after the chamber test. [IEC 60529 enclosure protection standard](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/2452)

Measure the cell, charger, boost, LED and case temperatures in the closed
vent-free case while the display runs and charges on the wall. Check both
the powered and battery-only configurations. Set the usable brightness
from the measured thermal limits. The complete enclosure seal and the
vent-free thermal limit remain unverified until the physical tests pass.
