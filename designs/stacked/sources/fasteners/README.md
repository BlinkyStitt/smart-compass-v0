# Narrow cap screws

Use the exact Grade5 Ti-6Al-4V stock and finished under-head targets in
[fasteners.json](../../fasteners.json). The52 selected cap screws use the same
nominal envelopes and clamp stacks. Keep all nuts and washers, the steel hinge,
keeper button screw, front countersunk screws and two long BlackPill screws.
No electrical circuit uses these mechanical screws as a return conductor.

Accu publishes the M2 head diameter3.8mm(+0/-0.18) and height2mm(+0/-0.14).
The8mm and16mm stock is fully threaded. The20mm stock has16mm minimum thread;
use it only for the17mm and17.7mm finished screws. AMPG2024catalogueC-7gives
nominal M3 head dimensions5.5x3mm andC-9lists ZS755M3X6/ZS755M3X10. Stock
availability, delivered dimensions and material identity require inspection.
No source stock-status, catalogue piece weight or strength statement is treated
as physical acceptance of this assembly.

TIMETTMC-0150, copyright2000,Table2 reports nominal Ti-6Al-4V density4.42g/cm3
at22C. This is reference alloy data, not a claim about a particular melt or a
guaranteed maximum density. The accounting owner applies the unchanged65..150%
engineering hardware allowance to the actual nominal CAD volume. Threads and
head sockets remain filled in that envelope. Finished mass is unmeasured.

Cut only where the listed target requires it. Deburr the cut and inspect full
nut engagement, head fit, socket condition and clamp seating. Keep the original
washers and spacers. Actual torque, galling, retention and strength remain
physical qualification items. Do not order or substitute from a printed mass
estimate alone. No purchase is part of this digital prototype delivery.
