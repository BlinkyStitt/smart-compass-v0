5. Sample acceptance — agree with the customer

The customer must supply the actual boards, strips, finished acrylic and fasteners for functional checks. If you cannot do these checks, return the labelled samples for customer testing. Report the machine, filament product/lot, nozzle, settings, measured dimensions and any defects.

  • Use actual M3 and M2 screws, plain nuts and washers. Check free screw passage, complete nut insertion and thread engagement, captive-nut pockets, controller mounting and screwdriver access. Keep the rear skin closed.
  • Test both bracket arrangements and mating frame/rear samples. Check that the rear can be removed independently.
  • Test the front clamp with the actual finished acrylic, 9 mm OD metal washers, and 9 mm OD × 6.4 mm ID soft washers cut from 0.020 inch Stockwell SG-30 GP silicone. Target 60% of measured free washer thickness. The metal washer must bear on the collar while the soft washer retains the sheet. Record the usable tightening limit.
  • Test all three ring curvatures and seats. Retain each ring at maximum permitted lift. Require 0.2–0.8 mm measured clearance below the front sheet and at least 0.5 mm actual LED clearance. Keep the $mirror_separation mm mirror separation and at least 18 mm radial cavity width.
  • Inspect the $outside_wall mm outside arc for voids, cracks and layer separation. Repeat installation and removal. Whole-ring handling still requires a full-size test.
  • Fit the three opaque light shields, actual strips and front-sheet sample. Reject exposed LED edges or light leaking above the rings. Check shield adhesion, sag, sheet clearance and removal with the ring. The shields use 0.06 mm tesa 7160 black PET tape; they are separate cut parts.
  • $optical_acceptance Use the customer's permitted LED power limit. The rendered appearance is an unmeasured illustration.

If a sample fails, stop the full order, correct the cause and repeat the affected test. Do not substitute material or relax an acceptance requirement without a new customer decision.

6. Full-size inspection and packing

  • Inspect cooled parts after brim removal. Reject cracks, incomplete walls, lifted edges, permanent distortion and layer separation. Do not use bolts to flatten a part.
  • Dry-assemble the complete set on a flat reference. Require joint gaps no greater than 0.3 mm, free screw passage, full nut engagement, retained rings, the specified optical gaps, and independent rear removal.
  • Label every part with its filename on removable packaging. Protect ring walls, collar stops, bearing faces and nut mouths. Report finished part masses after brim removal.
  • After assembly, the customer must weigh the complete pendant and necklace and repeat the suspension, sustained-load, cyclic-load and service tests. Print acceptance does not establish thermal or dust acceptance.
