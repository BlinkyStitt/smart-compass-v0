# Capacitor holder source records

These are retained original publisher PDFs. The index records their URLs,
revisions, hashes, byte counts and relevant conditions. Panasonic FR-A dated
1September2025 supplies the EEUFR1A102 dimensions and100kHz conditions.
Panasonic's mounting precautions require free valve space and case isolation;
they do not certify this holder. The RS PRO770-787 sheet gives nominal stock
thickness. Its linked publisher product page identifies polyester. Neither
source gives a supplied film thickness tolerance or an insulation certification.

The component checker verifies the retained PDF bytes. It does not measure the
supplied can, film, finished holder, retention, joints or thermal/fault behavior.
