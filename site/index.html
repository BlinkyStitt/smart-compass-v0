Runtime assumes 1.25 A total at 5 V, a provisional 37 Wh / 10 Ah LiPo pack and 85% conversion efficiency. The design target is 4 hours. Reserve, temperature, aging and cutoff losses reduce the calculated runtime. Brightness at this power cap remains unverified.

Battery access needs a revision. The current CAD uses a BatterySpace CU-MM109 pouch pack behind a screw-fastened rear cover. It is a fit candidate, not a purchase. The selected next revision uses one removable 26650 with spares and tool-free access, like the hats. The present compartment does not meet that requirement.

The build

Light you can wear.
Direction you can share.

The planned build: a 256-LED centre display, three additional LED strips and two controller boards.

The Evil Genius Labs Fibonacci256 forms the centre spiral. Separate strips make the circle halo, inner ring and outer ring. Adafruit Sparkle Motion will drive the lights; an STM32 BlackPill will read the sensors and handle the LoRa radio.

Navigation. We plan a compass mode that points north and toward live peers, plus a separate map mode showing peers relative to you. The goal is to navigate and exchange live locations without a phone or internet connection.

Light patterns. We plan geometric and microphone-reactive patterns across the centre display and strips. These simulations show a rotating rainbow with an eight-second colour cycle.

Inside the narrow design, a black acrylic mirror in front of the board provides the reflective rear surface. A clear two-way mirror closes the front. The inner LED ring sits between those mirrors.

The black acrylic also absorbs light. These renders include that loss: the estimated coated black sheet transmits 9%, while the finished clear front mirror transmits 30%. Light from the narrow board passes through both sheets; the inner ring bypasses the black sheet. The wide design keeps its black front and its extra loss. A mirror coating does not act as a diffuser, so the model treats diffusion separately.

These are CAD and optical simulations. The clear mirror split comes from supplier data; the coated black mirror and printed diffuser response remain estimates. Firmware, finished brightness, fit, heat and radio performance still need physical validation.
