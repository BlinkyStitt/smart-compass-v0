Runtime assumes 1.25 A total at 5 V, a provisional 37 Wh / 10 Ah LiPo pack and 85% conversion efficiency. The design target is 4 hours. Reserve, temperature, aging and cutoff losses reduce the calculated runtime. Brightness at this power cap remains unverified.

Battery access needs a revision. The current CAD uses a BatterySpace CU-MM109 pouch pack behind a screw-fastened rear cover. It is a fit candidate, not a purchase. The selected next revision uses one removable 26650 with spares and tool-free access, like the hats. The present compartment does not meet that requirement.

The build

Light you can wear.
Direction you can share.

Blinking lights and a friend finder.

The centre is an Evil Genius Labs Fibonacci256: 256 LEDs arranged in a spiral on a 166 mm board. The shape gives simple colour gradients a flowing, spiral appearance.

The electronics use Adafruit Sparkle Motion to drive the lights. An STM32 BlackPill reads GPS, motion and magnetic sensors, handles the LoRa radio, and sends updates over a wired link. It exchanges live locations with nearby peers without a phone or an internet connection.

Inside the narrow design, a black acrylic mirror in front of the board provides diffusion and the reflective rear surface. A clear two-way mirror closes the front. The inner LED ring sits between those mirrors.

The black acrylic also absorbs light. These renders include that loss: the estimated coated black sheet transmits 9%, while the finished clear front mirror transmits 30%.

These are CAD and optical simulations. The clear mirror split comes from supplier data; the coated black mirror and printed diffuser response remain estimates. Firmware, finished brightness, fit, heat and radio performance still need physical validation.
