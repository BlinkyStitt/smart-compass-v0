The build

Planned modes point north, point to a friend, or show nearby friends on a map.

The 256-LED Fibonacci256 spiral and three LED strips put 466 lights across the face and rim. Adafruit Sparkle Motion drives the lights. An STM32 BlackPill handles the sensors and radio.

A coated black acrylic mirror sits in front of the LED board. Light exits through a partly reflective front mirror.

Fixed-wiring battery swap

Switch off and disconnect external power. Use a protected XTAR 21700 6000 mAh button-top cell.

Charge the removed cell in a compatible 4.2 V Li-ion charger. Cover spare terminals. Stop if the unit is damaged or unusually hot. Switch off and remove the battery before connecting service USB.
