Smart compass.
Find your friends.

A light-up medallion designed to point you toward friends wearing one too. It will share locations by radio, so you won’t need a phone or internet connection. This 200 mm prototype combines a spiral of lights with an infinity mirror.

The build

How it works.

The planned compass mode points north or toward a friend. A map mode shows nearby friends relative to you. Location sharing uses LoRa radio.

The 256-LED Fibonacci256 spiral and three LED strips put 466 lights across the face and rim. Adafruit Sparkle Motion drives the lights; an STM32 BlackPill handles the sensors and radio.

Two mirrors create the infinity effect. A reflective black acrylic sheet sits in front of the LED board, and a partly reflective front mirror lets light out.

Planned light patterns include geometric animations and patterns that react to sound. Try the light-pattern experiments ↗

One removable battery

Tool-free battery access.

Switch off and disconnect external power. Open the door, release the keeper and pull the battery out with its ribbon. Insert a protected XTAR 21700 6000 mAh button-top cell as marked, then close the keeper and door. Switch on and press START.

The current design requires charging the battery outside the pendant. Use a compatible 4.2 V Li-ion charger and keep spare battery terminals covered. Stop using the unit if it is damaged or unusually hot. Remove the battery and leave the switch off before connecting USB for service.
